DATA VISUALISATION 2 • AFL ATTENDANCE • AUSTRALIA

AFL Nation

Where Australia’s footy passion really lives

AFL is called a national game, but crowd support is not evenly spread. This project uses 2025 attendance data to show where crowd power is strongest: clubs, stadiums, states, and season moments.

Question Where is AFL crowd power strongest?
Audience Australian sports fans and general readers
Data 2025 crowds, venues, clubs, rounds, and ladder results
Opening summary

1. The 2025 AFL crowd story at a glance

This visualisation follows a simple story: first, it shows the scale of AFL attendance in 2025; then it maps where crowd power is concentrated; finally, it tests whether crowd size is explained by clubs, venues, geography, or winning performance.

The season attracted millions of spectators. These headline figures set the scale before comparing clubs, venues, rounds, and geography.

Map view

2. AFL crowd power is concentrated in southern and western states

AFL venues are spread across Australia, but total attendance is not evenly distributed. State shading shows where 2025 AFL crowd power is strongest.

Club comparison

3. Collingwood led the AFL in average regular-season crowd size

Average home-and-away attendance shows which clubs pulled the strongest crowds in 2025. Traditional supporter bases and major fixtures help explain why some clubs sit far above the league average.

Venue ranking

4. The MCG dominates AFL venue attendance

Average crowd per game shows which stadiums consistently attracted the largest AFL audiences in 2025. This separates venue strength from simply hosting more matches.

Season trend

5. Finals crowds dwarf even the biggest home-and-away rounds

Average crowd per game changed across the 2025 AFL season, but finals created the clearest jump. The line shows home-and-away rounds, while the finals point shows the average across all finals games.

Home vs away

6. Home or away: where each club's crowds are bigger

Comparing home and away averages shows whether a club’s crowd strength comes from its own home base or from appearing in bigger away fixtures.

Performance link

7. Does winning bring bigger crowds?

Ladder position and wins help test whether crowd size is mainly driven by team performance. The comparison shows that popularity and geography can matter as much as winning.

Distribution

8. Most AFL venues draw small crowds — the MCG stands alone

Venue averages are not evenly spread. Most AFL venues sit in lower attendance bands, while the MCG separates itself as the clear high-crowd outlier.

Custom idiom

These small club cards compare home, away, and finals crowd averages using the same structure for every team. The dropdown lets readers focus on one crowd type at a time.

9B. AFL crowd heat tiles show each club’s crowd pattern

This custom heat-tile view compares each club across home, away, and finals crowds. Darker cells show larger average crowds, making each club’s crowd profile easy to scan.

Design method

How the visual encoding works

This project uses different visual marks and channels for different tasks. Bars and lollipops rank clubs and venues, colour shading maps geographic concentration, line position shows change across rounds, dots compare performance with attendance, and heat tiles reveal repeated club-level crowd patterns.

Final message

10. Final summary: AFL passion has a geography

AFL support is national, but crowd power is concentrated around a small set of high-drawing clubs, major stadiums, and football-heavy states.
